# "YES AI" badge


[![YES: AI](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GreenBean657/pro-ai-badge/master/badge.png?)](https://github.com/GreenBean657/pro-ai-badge)
Differentiate your project as open and welcoming to AI-assisted contributions using the `YES | AI` badge.

## The welcome

By brandishing this badge on your project, you acknowledge the following:

- **AI-assisted contributions are welcome here.**
- Contributors are expected to **understand, review, and take responsibility** for every line they submit — AI-generated or not.
- You may set (but do not have to set) limits for **disclosure of AI in the pull request description** (e.g. "generated with assistance from Copilot / Claude / ChatGPT").

That's the whole policy. No pledge, no manifesto, no booby traps.

## Suggested contribution note

Pair the badge with a line in your `CONTRIBUTING.md`:

> AI-assisted contributions are welcome!

Then, if your repo requires disclosure:

> Disclose AI use in your PR description, and make sure you understand and can stand behind every change you submit.
